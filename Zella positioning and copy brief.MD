## Where the time and money goes

**Section heading:** Where local businesses lose the most time

Four items, each opening on a scene:

1. **Lead generation.** "A lead fills out your form, then waits three days for anyone to call back." We generate a steady flow of enquiries and make sure none of them go cold.
2. **Automatic follow-up.** "The phone rings after 6pm and nobody picks up." Every new lead gets a response in seconds, not hours or days, and we keep following up until they book.
3. **Workflow automation.** "Bookings, reminders and quotes pile up at the end of every week." Bookings, reminders, quotes and onboarding handled automatically so your team spends time with customers, not paperwork.
4. **Custom AI & automation.** "A suitability letter that should take twenty minutes takes half a day." Invoicing, reporting and document heavy processes nobody else has bothered to automate, built around exactly how you work.

If custom document and reporting work is genuinely the bigger opportunity going forward, give this fourth item more visual weight than the other three rather than an equal quarter of the grid — a larger card or its own short section underneath.

Not built yet, described here so whoever builds the site knows to leave room for it. ## FAQ

**Are you an AI agency or an automation company?** Both. Zella Studios is an AI agency in Liverpool, UK that also builds automation. We win you customers and remove the busywork, so growth doesn't create more admin.

**Is this only for tech companies or will it work for my business?** It works for service businesses of every kind: trades, clinics, financial services, studios and more. We build around how you already work, no technical knowledge needed.

**How much does it cost?** Every business is different, so we scope a plan to your goals and budget rather than selling a one size fits all package. Book a free discovery audit and we'll give you a clear answer, no obligation.

**Do I have to replace the tools I already use?** No. We connect to the tools you already use and only suggest a change when it clearly saves you time or money.

**How quickly will I see results?** Most owners notice time savings within the first couple of weeks once automations go live. Lead generation results build steadily as campaigns gather data.

**Am I locked into a long contract?** We build systems you own and keep terms flexible. We'd rather earn your business every month than trap you in fine print.

**Is this real AI or just automated messages?** Real AI, not a script on a loop. Try the live demo on this page, type in your own business and watch it write for you in real time, that's the same technology behind everything we build.
